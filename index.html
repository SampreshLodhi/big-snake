Privacy Policy for Snake Puzzle

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains how the Snake Puzzle mobile game ("the app", "we") handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information (optional): if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and a unique user ID. This is used only to save and restore your game progress.
  • Game data: your level progress, coins, hints, owned skins, daily streak and purchase status. It is stored on your device and, if you sign in, in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore).
  • Advertising data: the app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and device and usage information to show and measure ads.
  • Purchase information: in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

How we use information

  • To run the game and save your progress across devices.
  • To show ads and measure ad performance.
  • To provide purchases you have made, and restore them.
  • To send a daily local reminder notification, if you allow notifications.

Third-party services

The app uses these services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore): https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Services and Google Play Billing: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Consent and ad choices

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and UK), the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android device settings.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above, as needed to operate the app.

Data retention and deletion

Game data stored in the cloud is kept while your account is active. To request deletion of your account and data, email us at the address below and we will delete it within 30 days. You can also uninstall the app to remove data stored on your device.

Security

We use reasonable measures, including Firebase security rules that restrict each user to their own data. No method of electronic storage is completely secure.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
